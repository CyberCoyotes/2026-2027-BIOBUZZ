Hood Wheel Ratio Test

Which hood wheel speed makes the ball spin the least?

Team: Date: Names:

The big idea

Our shooter squeezes the ball between two wheels: a big flywheel (about 4 inches) in front and a small hood wheel (about 2 inches) in the back. If the two wheel surfaces move at different speeds, the ball starts to spin. A spinning ball can curve or bounce in a way we do not want.

The small wheel has to turn faster to keep up with the big wheel. The number that tells us how much faster is the ratio:

Ratio = hood wheel speed ÷ flywheel speed
Example: flywheel 2000 RPM and ratio 2.00 → hood wheel = 2000 × 2.00 = 4000 RPM

Our guess: a ratio near 2.00 gives the least spin. Our job: test it, and find out if the best ratio stays the same when the flywheel goes faster or slower.

SAFETY — everybody must follow these

  • Eye protection on for everyone near the shooter.
  • Nobody stands in front of the shooter or in the path of the ball.
  • Hands, hair, sleeves and lanyards stay away from the wheels.
  • Wheels keep spinning after you stop them. Wait until they are completely stopped before you reach in.
  • Anyone can yell “STOP!” The Driver presses X right away.
  • Only a coach clears a jam.

Reading the screen

At Speed? true = OK to shoot.
CAPPED = the hood wheel cannot go that fast. Skip that row.
Lowest = the slowest each wheel went while the ball went through.

For every row of the data table

  1. Driver: set the flywheel RPM (B / A) and the ratio (D-pad) to match the row. Check the hood wheel RPM on the screen matches the table. If it says CAPPED, skip the row.
  2. Driver: press Y. Wait for At Speed? true.
  3. Feeder: load a ball. Driver: say “Shooting!” and flip the gate with the Left Trigger. Flip it back after the ball leaves.
  4. Video says “Shot 1”. Recorder fills in the spin code and the landing letter (see below).
  5. Wait for At Speed? true again. Repeat until you have 5 shots.
  6. Recorder: write the Lowest flywheel RPM and hood wheel RPM from the screen. Then the Driver presses Back.
  7. Go to the next row. (Changing a dial also resets the “lowest RPM”.)

How to write down a shot

Watch the slow-motion video. Follow the marker stripe from the moment the ball leaves until it lands.

Top box: spin code (a letter + a number)

BBackspin: the top of the ball turns back toward the robot
NNo spin: the stripe hardly moves (use N0)
TTopspin: the top of the ball turns forward toward the target

Number: 0 = none   1 = a little (stripe goes around less than 2 times)   2 = medium (2 to 6 times)   3 = a lot (more than 6). Examples: B2, N0, T1.

Bottom box: where it landed

SShort: landed before the target
OOn the target
LLong: landed past the target
The wheels keep spinning after you press X. Never reach in until they stop.
